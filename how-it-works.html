How To
Buy Data

Buy and receive mobile data in under 2 minutes. Here's exactly how it works.

Step-by-step
1
Pick Your Network
Tap MTN Ghana, Telecel, or AirtelTigo depending on whose SIM card you want data on.
2
Choose a Bundle
A list of available bundles will pop up. Select the size and price that works for you — from 1GB up to 10GB+.
3
Enter Recipient Number
Type the 10-digit number that should receive the data. You'll see the registered account name appear to confirm it's correct.
4
Pay with Paystack
Tap Buy Now. A secure Paystack checkout will open. You can pay via Mobile Money, Card, or Bank Transfer.
5
Receive Data Instantly
Once payment is confirmed, the data bundle is automatically sent to the phone number. Usually arrives within 2 minutes.

FAQs
What if I entered the wrong number? ▾
Unfortunately, data sent to the wrong number cannot be recalled. Always double-check the phone number before paying. The name verification feature helps you confirm the correct owner.
How long does delivery take? ▾
Delivery is usually instant to 2 minutes. During heavy traffic periods, it may take up to 30 minutes. If you wait longer than that, contact our support team on WhatsApp.
Is my payment secure? ▾
Yes. All payments are processed by Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Sci-Fi Data never sees or stores your card or MoMo PIN details.
Can I buy data for someone else? ▾
Yes! Enter their phone number in the Recipient Number field. The data will be sent directly to that number, not yours.
How do I check my order status? ▾
Go to the Orders tab and enter your transaction reference (starts with SF-) or the phone number you used. All your past orders will appear with their delivery status.
